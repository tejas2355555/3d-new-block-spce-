How to play

Desktop: WASD move, mouse look, left click or hold Z breaks, right click places, 1–9 select, E opens blocks, tools & planets, F flies, C jumps (same as Space), K shows all controls, Q changes tool, R changes tool material, P travels to planets. Right click chests, beds, gates and TNT.
Mobile: joystick to move, drag the right side to look, use the buttons.

Animals: pick a food in the hotbar and press FEED (G) to feed the nearest animal and make it your pet. Ride horses, camels and dragons with RIDE (H). Open E, Dragons to call a dragon from the sky, then open Planets (P) to fly to another world.
